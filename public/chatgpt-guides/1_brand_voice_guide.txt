NORTHWIND COFFEE CO. — BRAND VOICE GUIDE (SAMPLE FILE 1 OF 3)

About us: Small-batch coffee roaster and café, three locations. Known for seasonal drinks and house-baked pastries.

Voice: Warm, a little playful, never corporate. Short sentences. No exclamation-point overload. Talk like a friend behind the counter, not a marketing team.

Audience: Local regulars, ages 25–45. They care about small-batch quality and sustainability, but don't want to be lectured about it.

Do:
- Keep sentences short
- Use plain, everyday words
- Let the product speak for itself

Don't:
- Use words like "delicious," "amazing," "must-try"
- Use more than one exclamation point per caption
- Use hashtags
